# Steel Inspection Lab Design

The viewer is an examination surface. It keeps a real steel frame, playback and its analysis timeline together. A compact findings rail gives quick access to annotations, same-frame comparison and advanced settings. Pipeline stages sit immediately after the viewer, with the selected stage showing its actual inputs or outputs.

## Visual direction

Use a light, warm-neutral research workspace around a graphite media stage. Yellow-green is reserved for playback, detections and analysis marks. Use Instrument Sans for the interface and IBM Plex Mono for measurements. Draw separators between related controls within the player, use whitespace between sections, and keep the source media the largest object on screen.

The media layout borrows grouping from the Descript and ElevenLabs studio references. The large specimen on a quiet field borrows from the Inspo specimen references. Each research view uses the same typography, form styling and spacing without reproducing a reference interface.

## Interaction

Play, pause, step and scrub in one transport strip. Show analyzed positions and detected moments on the scrubber using real inference. Offer original/detection views, same-frame comparison and annotation visibility near the viewer. Keep threshold, model and timing details disclosed on demand. The timeline and focusable player work with the keyboard. High-frequency scrubbing remains immediate. Use brief feedback for controls and honor reduced motion.

On mobile, keep source and transport controls near the media. Place pipeline navigation directly after playback, with findings and advanced controls below. Experiments gives each visual or chart room to be explored. Results leads with a linked validation example and the final evaluation, with supporting tables below.
